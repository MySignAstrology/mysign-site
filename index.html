The difference

“Cancers are nostalgic” is not insight.

It's true of a twelfth of everyone alive, and you already knew your sign. What you didn't know is that you've said the same thing three times this year, and all three landed within a day of the same transit.

That's the whole product. The astrology gives it a voice and a reason. The memory is what makes it about you.

What this isn't.

Not a therapist, and not a crisis service. If things get heavy, call or text 988 — the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, free and 24/7.

It's for reflection, not prediction. It won't tell you how things end, and it won't make the irreversible decisions for you. Those are yours.
